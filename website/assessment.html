Six themes. Every system you nominate is scored against each one, with the evidence behind the score.

ThemeThe question answered
01Access & attribution Can every GxP action be traced to a unique person — or do shared logins exist?
02Audit trails Are they on, complete, reviewed, and reconstructable?

04Record integrity Can records be altered or backdated without a trace?
05Backup & retention Can data be deleted or overwritten without detection?
