# EVOLV Shorts · Ep.1 — "The 6pm Test Script"
### CSV Head × CSV Tester · vertical 9:16 · ~40s

**Series format:** two-person workplace conversation. The Head is
the calm, knowing peer; the second persona (here, the Tester)
carries the pain. Product facts land as casual throwaway lines,
never a pitch. Objection is handled *inside* the dialogue.

**Goal:** a CSV Tester watches, sees their own Tuesday, and books
a demo. **CTA:** book a 15-min demo.

**Characters (name them for realism):**
- **MAYA** — CSV Tester. Fried, end of day, dry humour.
- **SAM** — CSV Head. Coffee in hand, seen-it-all, unbothered.

## Production notes

- **Length:** keep under 45s; 38–40s is ideal for Reels/LinkedIn.
- **Captions:** burned-in, always (85% watch muted). Bold the
  punch words. Two-speaker colour coding helps (e.g. Maya white,
  Sam lime).
- **Pace:** fast cuts on the back-and-forth; hold ONE beat of
  silence after "how long have you known about this?" — the
  laugh/relatability lives in that pause.
- **Tone:** dry and real. Sam is *never* enthusiastic about the
  product; that's what makes it land. If a line sounds like an
  ad, cut it.
- **Setting:** open-plan office / lab-adjacent desk, end of day,
  warm low light. Coffee is a prop that gives Sam something to do.
- **Music:** minimal, low; let the dialogue breathe. Lift only on
  the CTA card.
- **The product glimpse (optional B-roll under Sam's lines
  0:14–0:20 and 0:20–0:31):** 2–3s screen capture of EVOLV drafting
  a script with citations + the traceability matrix filling in.
  Use the real platform — captures already in `website/assets/`.

## How to produce it (you're learning AI filmmaking)
1. **Two AI avatars** (HeyGen / similar) speaking the lines — fastest.
2. **AI-generated characters + voice** via the Seedance/cinema
   skills + ElevenLabs for voices — more cinematic, more control.
3. **Real footage** if you have two people — most authentic.
   Any of the three works; the script is the asset.
