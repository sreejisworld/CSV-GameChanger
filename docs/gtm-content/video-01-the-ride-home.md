# EVOLV Stories · Ep.1 — "The Ride Home"
### Brand film · vertical 9:16 · ~45s · voice-over-atmosphere

**Series premise:** real stories about working life and what it
costs. Never about EVOLV until the last frame. Nike logic —
people feel something; the logo is a quiet exhale at the end.

**Treatment:** the dialogue is *audio*. Visuals are impressionistic
— we rarely see mouths move. This plays to AI video's strengths
(atmosphere) and avoids its weakness (lip-sync). Moody, A24-ish.

**Funnel role:** top of funnel — reach + feeling + shareability.
Captured downstream by the website, the demo, and the
problem/solution shorts (see `video-01-csv-tester.md`).

## Locked script (audio) — "Late Again"

*Night. Maya drops into the back seat of a rideshare, loosens her
badge, exhales. The driver glances at her in the mirror. They've
done this ride before.*

> **DRIVER:** Long day?
> **MAYA:** Don't ask.
> **DRIVER:** That bad?
> **MAYA:** We had a meeting to recap the meetings we had all day.
> **DRIVER:** Oof.
> **MAYA:** Two hours.
> **DRIVER:** Hold on. The day's eight hours and the recap's two?
> **MAYA:** Yep.
> **DRIVER:** That's brutal.
> *(Maya laughs, but she's exhausted.)*
> **DRIVER:** Didn't you say last week you were done working nights?
> **MAYA:** That was before my manager found eight problems at six
> o'clock on a Friday.
> **DRIVER:** You still trying to get his job?
> **MAYA:** Kinda have to.
> **DRIVER:** Thought you hated the guy.
> **MAYA:** I do.
> **DRIVER:** Then why do you want to become him?
> *(Maya stares out the window.)*
> **MAYA:** …because that's how you move up.
> **DRIVER:** Doesn't seem like moving up.
> *(Beat.)*
> **MAYA:** What's that supposed to mean?
> **DRIVER:** Nothing.
> *(The car pulls up outside her apartment.)*
> **DRIVER:** You're home.
>
> *(Maya doesn't move. Cut to black.)*

**END CARD:**
> You can't automate bad managers.
> *(beat)*
> But the validation work keeping teams late?
> **EVOLV**
> Get your evenings back.

*(Optional A/B: "problems" → "deviations" for a CSV-insider cut;
end card can complete the echo with "That, you can." before the
logo.)*

*Caption style:* burned-in subtitles, clean sans, bottom third,
one line at a time, dim so they don't fight the mood. 85% watch
muted — the story must read on captions alone.

## ElevenLabs voice direction
- **MAYA:** female, late-20s/30s, tired, flat affect, a dry edge
  under the exhaustion. Not sad-acting — *depleted*. Slow.
- **DRIVER:** male, warm, unhurried, genuinely curious — the
  innocent who asks the obvious question. Never smug.
- **Pace:** leave the beats. The silence after "…but you want to
  become that person" and before "You're home" is the film.

## Music
- Minimal. A single sustained pad / soft piano, low in the mix.
- No drums. Let rain + city be part of the score.
- The only lift is a gentle swell as the END CARD fades in.
