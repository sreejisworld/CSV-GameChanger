## The five things that matter

1. **Brief → Requirements in minutes** — paste a paragraph, get
   UR/FRs with page-level GAMP 5 citations and risk classification.
2. **Risk-adaptive test authoring** — High risk gets full scripted
   depth; Low gets exploratory charters. Per-step regulatory
   citations (21 CFR 11 · EU Annex 11 · ICH Q9) — unique in market.
3. **Living traceability** — one row per UR: risk → tests → runs →
   defects → release state. Updates itself as work happens.
4. **Inspector-grade audit trail** — every AI decision writes a
   hash-linked Logic Archive an auditor can replay. "Show me how
   the AI decided that" has a literal answer.
5. **Bounded autonomy** — agent passports declare what each AI can
   never do. Five deployment shapes refused outright (AI signing,
   batch release, CAPA closure, clinical decisions, unsigned
   validated-record writes).
