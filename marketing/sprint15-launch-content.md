# Post 3 — Sprint 15.2 (Adhoc Step Insertion)

## LinkedIn Post

⚡ The most expensive 30 seconds in CSV: the tester is mid-run,
spots a boundary case the script doesn't cover, and has TWO bad
choices —
(a) Skip it and hope the inspector doesn't ask, or
(b) Stop the run, raise a CR, re-author the script, re-execute
    everything from step 1.

Neither is acceptable. So testers usually pick (a) and pretend.

We just shipped Adhoc Step Insertion in EVOLV.

Here's what it does:
✅ "+ Insert adhoc step here" button between any two execution rows
✅ Tester fills in title, instruction, expected result, and a
   mandatory REASON (audit trail)
✅ Step inserts inline with hierarchical numbering (3.1, 3.2) so
   prior step results stay valid
✅ Tagged source='tester-adhoc' — distinguishable in ALCOA reports,
   PDF exports, and the QA review screen
✅ Captures who inserted it, when, and why — automatically

What this means for biotech & pharma startups:
→ Your senior testers stop pretending. Edge cases get tested.
→ Defects that would have shipped get caught
→ Audit-distinguishable means the inspector sees BOTH the planned
   script AND the tester's exploratory additions — and trusts you
   more, not less
→ FDA CSA explicitly endorses tester-in-the-loop testing. EVOLV
   makes it auditable.

Why we built EVOLV:
2 decades in CSV. Watched too many testers tick "Pass" on a step
that didn't cover the boundary they knew was risky — because the
