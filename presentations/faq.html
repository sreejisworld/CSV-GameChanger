📋 Compliance & Regulatory 6 questions
Is EVOLV itself a validated system under GAMP 5? ▼
EVOLV is developed following GAMP 5 principles. Every function in the codebase is tagged with a :requirement: URS-X.Y docstring linking it to a User Requirements Specification. The repository contains a complete URS Traceability Index mapping every requirement to its implementation — covering 80+ URS items across 15 agents and 9 API routers.
"We validate ourselves. EVOLV's own URS traceability index is the starting point for any IQ/OQ/UAT package we provide to customers."
Does EVOLV produce 21 CFR Part 11 compliant records? ▼
Yes. Every audit record includes: timestamp (UTC), user ID, action, decision logic, compliance impact, and a SHA-256 tamper-evident reasoning hash. The audit trail is append-only — no record can be modified or deleted. PDF exports include a Manifestation of Signature page with signer name, timestamp, and meaning of signature, as required by 21 CFR Part 11 § 11.50 and § 11.70. The AI Governance Hub Override Ledger is separately SHA-256 hashed and immutable.
What GAMP 5 software categories does EVOLV support? ▼
EVOLV supports Categories 1, 3, 4, and 5. The Plan phase captures your system's GAMP category and adjusts the risk matrix, testing strategy, and required documentation accordingly. Category 4 (configured) and Category 5 (custom/bespoke) systems trigger the full OQ/UAT testing workflow. Category 3 (non-configured) can use supplier-provided documentation with informal testing.
How does EVOLV handle the FDA's 2022 CSA guidance? ▼
The DeltaAgent implements all three CSA testing strategies: Unscripted (low risk — exploratory charters), Hybrid (medium risk — scripted + unscripted), and Rigorous Scripted (high risk — formal OQ and UAT). Test type selection (Informal, Formal OQ, Formal UAT) is determined automatically from the risk matrix output — not manual judgment — and logged to the audit trail with the rationale.
Can EVOLV outputs be submitted directly to regulators? ▼
EVOLV generates structured, formatted, inspection-ready documentation — URS PDFs, Validation Reports, Transparency Reports — designed to support regulatory submissions. However, your qualified person / CSV lead remains responsible for final review and submission. EVOLV accelerates and governs the process; it does not replace professional regulatory judgment. Think of it as a governed AI assistant, not an autonomous regulatory agent.
How does EVOLV track regulatory version changes (e.g. new GAMP 5 edition)? ▼
Each Pinecone knowledge base chunk is tagged with a reg_version derived from the source PDF filename (e.g. GAMP5_Rev2, 21CFR11_Rev1). When a new regulatory document is ingested via the Ingestor Agent, a new version tag is created and flagged. Every URS document cites the exact regulatory version used in its rationale — creating a complete traceability chain from requirement to regulatory source to document version.
🛡️ AI Governance & Human-in-the-Loop 5 questions
What happens if the AI makes a wrong decision? ▼
That's exactly what the AI Governance Hub is for. Every AI decision — URS generation, risk classification, test script generation — is queued for human review before it advances. The reviewer can Approve, Override (with documented reason and new value), or Reject. If overridden, the change is permanently recorded in the Override Ledger with the reviewer's name, role, timestamp, reason, and a SHA-256 hash. The AI cannot self-approve any output.
"The AI proposes. The human decides. The system records everything."
Is the AI making binding decisions or just suggestions? ▼
Suggestions only. EVOLV uses a strict "propose and review" model. The AI generates a draft; a human approves it. Architecturally, every pending decision requires an explicit human action (approve / override / reject) to change its status. There is no auto-approval pathway. If a decision is not reviewed, it remains in "pending" status and blocks downstream workflow advancement.
How do I prove to an FDA inspector that a human reviewed the AI output? ▼
The Override Ledger and Audit Timeline provide exactly this evidence. For any AI decision, you can show: who reviewed it, their role, when they reviewed it (UTC timestamp), what they approved or changed, and why. The Transparency Report generates a per-requirement PDF of this complete chain in under 5 seconds — formatted for inspector review with a 21 CFR Part 11 compliance statement at the bottom.
What AI models does EVOLV use? ▼
OpenAI GPT-4o for reasoning-heavy tasks (URS generation, SMART requirement refinement, intelligence analysis). text-embedding-3-small for semantic search against the regulatory knowledge base (Pinecone). The Pinecone index stores 10,000+ chunks from GAMP 5, CSA guidance, 21 CFR Part 11, and ICH Q9 — all with page-level citations and regulatory version tags.
Can EVOLV's AI reasoning be audited? ▼
Yes — through two mechanisms. First, every AI output includes the AI Reasoning and the specific GAMP 5 page citation used to generate it (visible in the Governance Hub). Second, the IntegrityManager writes a Logic Archive (JSON) for every significant AI action — a tamper-evident file containing the full inputs, intermediate reasoning steps, and outputs, cross-referenced to the CSV audit trail row via SHA-256 hash.
🔒 Data & Security 3 questions
Where is our validation data stored? ▼
Currently, EVOLV runs on-premise (locally or on your cloud VM). Validation data is stored in your file system (output/ directory). No validation data is stored on WingstarTech servers. The only external calls are: OpenAI API (requirement text for embedding/generation) and Pinecone (embeddings for similarity search — no raw text stored externally). A cloud-hosted multi-tenant version with full data isolation is on the roadmap for Q3 2026.
What data is sent to OpenAI? ▼
Requirement text submitted by the user is sent to OpenAI for embedding and generation. No patient data, clinical data, or proprietary system configuration should be entered as requirements — EVOLV is for validation documentation generation, not clinical data processing. We recommend your internal data classification policy be reviewed before submitting any text to AI processing, consistent with your OpenAI data processing agreement.
Can EVOLV run in an air-gapped environment? ▼
Partially today, fully on roadmap. The core risk assessment (RiskStrategist), test script generation (DeltaAgent), and SMART refinement (deterministic mode) work without internet access. URS generation requires Pinecone + OpenAI. A fully air-gapped mode with a local LLM (Ollama) and local vector store is on the roadmap for Q4 2026.
🔌 Integration & Implementation 4 questions
How long does it take to implement EVOLV? ▼
Day 1: run docker-compose up. The platform starts across three ports (React: 5173, Streamlit: 8501, FastAPI: 8000). For a proof-of-concept with your own requirements, you need: API keys (OpenAI, Pinecone) and your regulatory PDFs for ingestion. First URS generation: typically within 2 hours of setup. Full production deployment with your own regulatory document library: 1–2 days.
Does EVOLV integrate with our LIMS / ERP / ServiceNow? ▼
The FastAPI backend has a ServiceNow webhook endpoint (POST /webhook/sn-change) that accepts Change Requests and triggers automated risk assessment. LIMS/ERP integration is via REST API — EVOLV exposes a full OpenAPI 3.0 specification (documented at /docs and /redoc). Custom integrations can be built against these endpoints. An MCP server also exposes EVOLV agents as native Claude tools for AI-to-AI workflows.
Can we use our own regulatory documents and SOPs? ▼
Yes. The Ingestor Agent accepts PDF uploads and ingests them into the Pinecone knowledge base with metadata tagging (source document, page number, regulatory version). Once ingested, your SOPs, site-specific guidance, and internal standards become part of the knowledge base that AI agents query when generating URS and verification rationale — alongside the built-in GAMP 5 and CSA content.
We need to validate EVOLV itself before using it. How does that work? ▼
Correct — EVOLV is GAMP 5 Category 4 configured software in your environment. We provide a complete IQ/OQ/UAT package as part of onboarding: Installation Qualification protocol, Operational Qualification test scripts (generated by EVOLV itself), and UAT acceptance criteria. Our own URS Traceability Index is the starting point. Validation of EVOLV is typically completed in 2–5 days depending on your internal review process.
"EVOLV validates your systems. We also validate EVOLV. We use our own platform to do it."
