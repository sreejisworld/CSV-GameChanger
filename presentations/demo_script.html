💬 Common Objections & Responses
"We already have a QMS / validation tool."▼
EVOLV doesn't replace your QMS. It generates the validation documentation that goes into it. Think of it as the AI authoring layer on top of your existing process — the URS, risk assessment, and test evidence that your QMS stores. Most teams use EVOLV to generate, then import into Veeva, MasterControl, or their SharePoint-based system.
"We need to validate EVOLV before we can use it."▼
Correct — and we've prepared for that. EVOLV is GAMP 5 Category 4 configured software. We provide a complete IQ/OQ/UAT package including installation qualification, OQ test scripts (generated by EVOLV itself), and UAT acceptance criteria. Our own URS traceability index is the starting point. Validation of EVOLV typically takes 2–5 days.
"What about data privacy? We can't send our data to OpenAI."▼
EVOLV runs on your infrastructure — on-premise or your private cloud VM. No validation data touches WingstarTech servers. The only external calls are: requirement text to OpenAI for embedding (no clinical or patient data), and embeddings to Pinecone. You control the data at all times. Enterprise OpenAI agreements with data processing addendums are supported.
"The AI will hallucinate and give us wrong regulatory citations."▼
Every AI output cites a specific GAMP 5 page — verifiable. If the AI can't find supporting regulatory text with a similarity score above threshold, it rejects the generation entirely and tells you why — rather than making something up. And critically: every output requires human review before it advances. The AI cannot hallucinate its way into a finalised document without a human seeing it first.
"We have our own SOP templates and internal standards."▼
EVOLV can ingest your SOPs, site-specific guidance, and internal standards into the knowledge base. Once ingested, they become part of the regulatory context the AI queries — alongside GAMP 5 and CSA. Your internal standards get cited alongside the external regulatory text.
"What's the pricing?"▼
We're in early-access with select pharma and biotech partners. Pricing is project-based or enterprise SaaS depending on your validation portfolio volume. The fastest path: run a pilot on one real project — that gives us both the data we need to have a pricing conversation that makes sense for your context.
